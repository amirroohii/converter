How to Convert PowerPoint to PDF

Follow these simple steps to convert your PowerPoint presentations to PDF format:

  1. Select the PowerPoint file (.ppt or .pptx) you want to convert by dragging and dropping it into the upload area or by clicking to browse your files.
  2. Once your file is selected, click the "Convert to PDF" button to initiate the conversion process.
  3. Wait for the conversion to complete. The duration may vary depending on the size and complexity of your presentation.
  4. After the conversion is finished, a download link for your PDF file will appear.
  5. Click the download link to save your newly converted PDF file to your device.

Note: Our tool supports both .ppt and .pptx formats. The converted PDF will preserve the layout, formatting, and animations of your original PowerPoint presentation to the extent possible in a static PDF format.

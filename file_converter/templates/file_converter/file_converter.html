How to Convert Word to PDF

Follow these simple steps to convert your Word documents to PDF format:

  1. Select the Word document (.doc or .docx) you want to convert by dragging and dropping it into the upload area or by clicking to browse your files.
  2. Once your file is selected, click the "Convert to PDF" button to start the conversion process.
  3. Wait for the conversion to complete. This usually takes a few seconds, depending on the size of your document.
  4. After the conversion is finished, a download link for your PDF file will appear.
  5. Click the download link to save your newly converted PDF file to your device.

Note: Our tool supports both .doc and .docx formats. The converted PDF will maintain the formatting of your original Word document as closely as possible.

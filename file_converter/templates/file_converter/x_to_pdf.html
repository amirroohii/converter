How to Convert Excel to PDF

    Follow these simple steps to convert your Excel files to PDF format:

  1. Select the Excel file (.xls or .xlsx) you want to convert by dragging and dropping it into the upload area or by clicking to browse your files.
  2. Once your file is selected, click the "Convert to PDF" button to start the conversion process.
  3. Wait for the conversion to complete. This usually takes a few seconds, depending on the size of your file.
  4. After the conversion is finished, a download link for your PDF file will appear below.
  5. Click the download link to save your newly converted PDF file to your device.

Note: Our tool supports both .xls and .xlsx formats. The converted PDF will maintain the formatting of your original Excel file as closely as possible.

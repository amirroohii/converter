How to Convert Your Images

Follow these simple steps to convert your images to different formats:

  1. Select the image file you want to convert by dragging and dropping it into the upload area or by clicking to browse your files.
  2. Choose the desired output format from the options provided (e.g., JPG, PNG, WebP).
  3. Click the "Convert" button to start the conversion process.
  4. Once the conversion is complete, a download link will appear for your converted image.
  5. Click the download link to save your converted image to your device.

Note: Our tool supports various image formats including JPG, PNG, WebP, and more. You can convert between these formats easily and quickly.
